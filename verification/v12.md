# V12 verification

## Changed behavior

- Catalog rotation draws one opaque source view. Fractional input never overlaps two silhouettes, including the 360-degree seam. Poster visibility switches immediately.
- Momentum, auto rotation, reduced motion, pinch/pan cancellation, loading recovery and idle cleanup remain available.
- The reference-led MOVE model retains five layers and whole, exploded and skeleton modes. Enlarged layer spacing accommodates the rebuilt sole and toe bumper.
- Versioned script links and a new model/poster filename prevent the previous asset being reused from cache.

## Evidence

- `spin.test.cjs`: one complete frame at fractional positions and seam crossings; computed opacity/blending/poster styles; actual controller momentum, gestures and recovery.
- `product-details.test.cjs`: all 12 products, three languages and 12 distinct viewpoints; rotation/pinch/pan cooperation and component details.
- `model.test.mjs`: actual GLB parsing, open collar and insole, real geometry, material UVs/normal map, batching from 421 meshes to 14, five separated layers and exact reassembly.
- `3d-gestures.test.mjs`: actual OrbitControls pinch/pan/rotation logic, 12× zoom and tap cancellation.
- `source.test.py`: local assets, translations, anchors, alpha, 12 distinct atlas cells per product and unchanged resource budgets.
- Offscreen model asset renders inspected assembled, from the reverse side, exploded and in skeleton view. These use the same mesh checkpoint as the GLB export.

## Limits

No browser preview capability was available in the managed environment. DOM and geometry checks do not measure physical-device GPU output, touch latency, frame rate or visual page layout. The turntables still have 12 generated viewpoints, not continuous 3D. The anatomy model is an approximation from one photograph, not a scan or real construction specification. The WooCommerce trial ZIP was not rebuilt.
